# Python4LabRats
## Why a lab rat shouldn't be afraid of Python
A (modern, well-designed) graphical user interface (GUI) feels natural and intuitive to use. Such programs do exist for Sanger assembly, but they come with drawbacks. Some of them include...
- They may not be available for Windows, Mac, *and* Linux.
- They may not be open-source.
- They may not be easily scaled up for large projects.
- They may require a human to regularly provide manual inputs.
- They may not natively record every step of the process.

Programming languages allow the creation of **easily reproducible, highly modifiable, highly automatable** data workflows that are **easily saved for posterity and shared between researchers.**

A lab rat may want to sit with the previous sentence for a moment. Since their first day in their first lab class, the lab rat has been trained to obsessively keep meticulous notes. They may be recalling the way that their every smudge, every hasty scrawl, every scratched-out error was red-lined by hawk-eyed TAs. They may be thinking:

👉👈 ...is for me?

Yes, brave little lab rat.

Is for you.
